About

This blog is a personal project in which I explore the works of R. A. Lafferty in my spare time. Lafferty was a devout Roman Catholic. My blog tries to read him on his own terms and be clear when I break with him. I read Lafferty on the assumption that his difficulties have meaning and that he is trying to convert one’s sense of reality. I also read him as a deeply esoteric writer. My working interpretation is that his fiction builds a deliberately coded, systematic theological architecture, using science fiction to carry difficult, orthodox, and radical metaphysical arguments.

A typical post might include three parts: a summary, an interpretation, and some visual aids or other curiosities to help readers approach Lafferty’s writing in their own way. I mostly love this thing, but I won't lie for it, nor will I run reputational cover. I am critical of how Lafferty has been handled in scholarship, especially on the issue of his antisemitism and Holocaust denial.

The Resources section features tools I built for my own use as I learned Lafferty over six months. They're really learning documents, but I thought others might find them useful too. If you are brand new to Lafferty, some tips can be found on the Getting Started with Lafferty post and the one on The Whole Lafferty.

You can reach me at mumbrumber101@gmail.com. Reach out if you want to talk about Lafferty or if I can help with your own Lafferty research.
